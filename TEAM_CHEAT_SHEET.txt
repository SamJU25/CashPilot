Hey team! Here is a 3-minute summary of what we are building, why it matters,
and the key concepts you need to know for development and our pitch.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT ARE WE BUILDING?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An "Autonomous AI CFO and Financial Participant for SMEs (Small & Medium Enterprises)".
It connects to a small business's bank transactions and invoices, predicts cash
shortages before they happen, and safely drafts financing solutions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. AI AGENTS IN 60 SECONDS (WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Chatbot vs. Agent:
  - A chatbot (like standard ChatGPT) only generates text.
  - An AI Agent has "hands": it reads databases, runs Python code, calls APIs,
    and takes actions inside strict safety boundaries.

* Why Multi-Agent?
  - A real business has an accountant, a backend engineer, and a security officer.
  - Our project splits work into specialized agents (FinTech math squad, Backend
    squad, Security squad). This prevents hallucinations and errors.

* The Golden Rule of Financial AI:
  - The AI NEVER guesses financial math in text.
  - The AI writes Python code, and Python computes the exact numbers deterministically.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SME FINANCE 101 (THE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Why Profitable Small Businesses Go Bankrupt:
  - A business can make $50,000 in paper profit this month, but if customers pay
    in 60 days (Net 60 terms) and payroll ($20,000) is due tomorrow, the bank
    account is empty.
  - This timing gap between paying expenses and collecting cash is called the
    "Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)".

* Why Traditional Banks Reject SMEs:
  - Banks demand physical collateral (factories, buildings) and 3 years of audited
    statements. Small businesses don't have that.

* Our Solution (The Digital Underwriting / WeBank Model):
  - Instead of waiting for paper audits, we look at real-time cash flows:
    daily bank balance trends, revenue stability, tax filings, and unpaid invoices.
  - We warn the business owner before payroll bounces and recommend tailored financing
    (invoice factoring, credit line, or revenue-based financing).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW WE WIN THE HACKATHON (THE 3 SCORING CRITERIA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Judges do NOT want another generic ChatGPT wrapper. They grade 3 areas:

1. Task Completion (40%):
   - The Copilot actually ingests transactions and calculates real financial metrics:
     Cash Runway, Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), and Cash Conversion Cycle.

2. Defensive Security (30%) — OUR BIGGEST ADVANTAGE:
   - We enforce a "Three-Tier Permission Gate":
     * Tier 1 (Read-Only): Checking balances, calculating ratios -> AI runs autonomously.
     * Tier 2 (Action Drafting): Preparing loan requests or invoice drafts -> AI drafts,
       human confirms.
     * Tier 3 (High Stakes): Sending real money or signing loans -> STRICTLY LOCKED
       behind Human 2-Factor Authentication (2FA). The AI cannot move funds alone.

3. Technical Innovation (30%):
   - Replacing slow paper loan applications with real-time cash-flow intelligence.
   - Optional tamper-proof verification to prevent invoice double-financing.
